# Third-party notices

The application may integrate with external fantasy-football providers and media sources. Adapters must preserve source attribution and must not imply that provider data is covered by this repository's MIT license.

## Experimental inputs

Undocumented Sleeper RotoWire projections and ESPN imagery are not currently cleared for publication. They are local experimental inputs only until the project's publication and licensing rules are resolved. Keep such material out of releases, public fixtures, and the static distribution bundle.

When a source is cleared, add its terms, attribution requirements, and the exact integration surface here. When a source cannot be cleared, retain only code that can operate without distributing its content.
